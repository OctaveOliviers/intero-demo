Questions, answered

What is Melodic?

On-premise AI agents that extract and structure clinical data from your existing systems and automate the manual workflows around it, with a clinician in the loop.

What can it automate?

Mandatory, labour-intensive admin: clinical audits, registry and reporting returns, data extraction and cleaning, and ad-hoc data requests. Audits are where most teams start.

Do we have to integrate it with our systems?

No. The agents navigate the systems you already run, so there's no lengthy integration and no infrastructure change.

Does any patient data leave the hospital?

No. Everything runs on-premise, inside your network. Sensitive data never leaves the hospital.

Is there human oversight?

Yes. A clinician reviews and signs off on every output, and the agents learn from each correction.
