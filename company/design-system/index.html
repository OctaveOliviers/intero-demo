Typography

Display: Satoshi (700/900). Body: Inter (400/500/600).

Display 72 / 1.05 / -0.03em

Heading 32 / 1.15 / -0.02em

Body 16 / 1.55 regular for narrative text.

Meta 13 / 0.14em uppercase for labels only.
